## 7. Upload settings checklist
- **Category:** Education
- **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids" (general audience). Choose "Made for kids" only if you target children; that disables comments and the notification bell.
- **Language:** Hindi. Also upload `output/Hindi_Subtitles.srt` under Subtitles (indexed captions help search).
- **Altered or synthetic content:** the narration uses an AI (TTS) voice. On YouTube, answer the disclosure question per its current policy; a realistic synthetic voice generally should be disclosed.
- **Chapters:** they appear automatically from the timestamps in the description.
- **Length note:** the video is 3:21. Shorts and Reels are currently limited to about 3 minutes, so on YouTube it posts as a regular (vertical) video rather than a Short. For a Short or Reel, ask for a cut under 3:00.
- **Best posting time:** for Indian audiences, typically evenings 6–9 PM IST. Check YouTube Analytics → "When your viewers are on YouTube" once you have data.
